## Small capacity-building structure

This dedicated animal shelter is designed as a low-cost, early-game way to expand the province's sustainable animal economy.

It grants:

- +20 Animal Capacity
- +0.25 Gold/Month
- +0.3 Animal Influence
- cost: 50 Gold

This is intentionally cheap so it can be built repeatedly and used as a scalable early development tool for an Aquis province.
