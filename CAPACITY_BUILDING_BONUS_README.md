# ==============================
# Capacity Bonus Notes
# ==============================

## Building capacity bonus

Temple and Animal Holding structures now grant a +30 capacity bonus each.

This means:

- every Aquis temple can help sustain more animals
- every animal-focused holding increases the province's animal capacity
- the capacity system becomes more meaningful as the player expands their animal economy

## Why this matters

This encourages players to build in a way that supports their animal economy rather than simply stacking raw Animal Influence buildings.

The result:

- more animal holdings = more capacity
- better management = better Animal Influence gain
- over-capacity remains a strategic risk
